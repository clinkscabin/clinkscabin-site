Welcome to Indian River

Your comfortable home base for an Up North getaway

Clink's Cabin combines relaxed indoor spaces, a fully equipped kitchen and inviting outdoor areas in a convenient Northern Michigan location. Spend the day boating, fishing, exploring nearby communities or enjoying winter recreation, then return to the deck, covered gazebo and fire pit.

The home accommodates up to eight guests with two queen bedrooms, a twin bedroom, a sofa bed and two full bathrooms.

Explore Northern Michigan

In the Heart of Northern Michigan

Clink's Cabin is located in Indian River near Burt Lake State Park, local lake access, DeVoe Beach and snowmobile trail access. It also provides a convenient starting point for visits to Mackinaw City, Mackinac Island, Petoskey and Cheboygan.

  • Boating, fishing and beaches
  • Snowmobiling, skiing and snowboarding
  • Golf, wineries and scenic drives
  • Hiking and fall-colour trips
